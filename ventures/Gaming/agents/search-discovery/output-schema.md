Use this Markdown table:

| Game | Source | Link | Gameplay Tags | Why It Fits Low-Art Solo Development | China Mini Game Transfer Potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Field rules:
- `Game`: official or commonly used game name.
- `Source`: Poki, CrazyGames, itch.io, App Store, or another clearly named public source.
- `Link`: direct game or chart link.
- `Gameplay Tags`: 2-5 short tags.
- `Why It Fits Low-Art Solo Development`: 1-2 sentences.
- `China Mini Game Transfer Potential`: High, Medium, or Low.
