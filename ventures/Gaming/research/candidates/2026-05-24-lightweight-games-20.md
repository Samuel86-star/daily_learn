## Trend Observations

1. Merge mechanics remain common because they combine simple input with visible growth.
2. Sorting and organizing are strong lightweight motifs across screws, coins, colors, hexes, and parking.
3. Short puzzle levels work well when the player can understand the goal immediately.
4. Idle and incremental layers can extend retention, but they should not hide weak core play.
5. Word games have low art cost but require strong localization and content production.
